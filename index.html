About the company

A private property company with a simple focus.

OF & H PROPERTIES LTD is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 16268810. It was incorporated on 21 February 2025.

The company buys residential property to hold for the long term and lets the homes it owns directly to tenants. From time to time it may also sell a property from its own portfolio.

The company acts only as the owner and landlord of its own homes. It does not act as a letting agent, and it does not let or manage property on behalf of other landlords.

Its registered office is at Bartle House, 9 Oxford Court, Manchester M2 3WQ.

What we do

Buying, letting and looking after our own homes.

Everything the company does relates to residential property it owns itself.

Property acquisition

The company buys residential property to hold for the long term as part of its own portfolio. Vendors and estate agents with suitable residential property are welcome to get in touch.

Residential lettings

Homes owned by the company are let directly to tenants. When one of our homes becomes available to rent, it is advertised at that time.

Property care

As landlord, the company looks after the upkeep of its own properties and deals with repairs, so that its homes stay in good order for the people living in them.

Enquiries

One point of contact for tenants, prospective tenants, vendors and agents. Written enquiries can be sent to the company at its registered office, shown in the contact section.

Our approach

Straightforward, and in it for the long term.

  • Direct dealings

    Tenants, vendors and agents can deal directly with the company that owns the property.

  • Long-term ownership

    The company buys homes to keep and let, rather than to trade quickly.

  • Homes that are looked after

    As the owner, the company has a direct interest in keeping each of its properties well maintained.
